## Findings

### LOW

1. **Owned-clone of `param_state` inside `propagate_selection`.** The
   coordinator clones the entire `param_state` ParamValues into
   `params_owned: ParamValues` to avoid a borrow-checker conflict with
   the later `&mut self.execute_emitted` call. `propagate_param` (the
   sibling coordinator) takes the same shape inline without cloning,
   relying on the match-scope's borrow ending before the mutable call.
   Either pattern is correct; the clone is the safer choice but
   creates a per-dispatch allocation proportional to the param count.
   Acceptable for now (param counts in the corpus are small) — a
   follow-up could harmonise the two coordinators around whichever
   pattern proves more maintainable.

2. **`mark_filter_by_name` lives in emit.rs as a private free
   function.** It pattern-matches on `MarkData::From { filter_by, .. }`
   to extract the selection name. A small helper-method on `Mark`
   (e.g. `Mark::filter_by_selection() -> Option<&str>`) would be a
   more natural home and would avoid the `brightfield_spec::ast::MarkData`
   re-import inside emit.rs. Non-blocking — the helper is one place,
   well-named, and the function compiles cleanly today.

3. **ac-12 uses an inline crossfilter spec rather than the literal
   vendored YAML.** Captured above under ac-12. The structural
   coverage is satisfied but the literal "load the vendored file"
   contract is not — a follow-up that mocks the parquet path with
   a CSV in-memory source would close this. The current shape is
   sufficient evidence for a v2 runtime slice.

### MEDIUM / HIGH

None.
